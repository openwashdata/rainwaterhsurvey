# Rainwater Harvesting and Groundwater Recharge Household Survey (BASEflow, 2025)

This dataset contains household-level survey data collected in 2025
under the BASEflow monitoring and data systems using the mWater digital
data collection platform. The survey was conducted across selected
communities to assess household water access conditions, perceptions of
groundwater systems, and adoption of rainwater harvesting (RWH)
practices.

## Usage

``` r
rainwaterhsurvey
```

## Format

A tibble with 479 rows and 18 variables

- district:

  Name of district where the household is located

- community:

  Name of the local community or village of the respondent

- primary_water_source:

  Main source of water used by the household for drinking and domestic
  purposes

- water_scarcity:

  Whether the household experiences water scarcity

- scarcity_months:

  Months of the year when the household experiences water scarcity

- perceived_water_source:

  Respondents perception of where their household water originates from

- belief_underground_rivers:

  Whether the respondent believes that underground rivers supply
  boreholes and wells

- belief_groundwater_exhausted:

  Whether the respondent believes that drying of boreholes indicates
  complete depletion of groundwater

- belief_groundwater_replenished:

  Whether the respondent believes that groundwater can be replenished or
  naturally refilled

- belief_recharge_detectable:

  Respondents view on whether it is possible to detect or know if
  groundwater is being replenished

- rwh_practice:

  Whether the household practices rainwater harvesting

- rwh_barrier:

  Main reason why the household does not practice rainwater harvesting

- rwh_use:

  Primary uses of harvested rainwater within the household

- belief_rwh_recharge:

  Respondents belief that rainwater harvesting contributes to
  groundwater recharge

- belief_rwh_expensive:

  Respondents belief that rainwater harvesting requires expensive
  technology or is unaffordable

- belief_rwh_unreliable:

  Respondents belief that rainwater harvesting is ineffective due to
  rainfall variability or unreliability

- recharge_solutions:

  Respondents suggestions on methods or practices that can help
  replenish groundwater

- rwh_support_needed:

  Types of support, information, or interventions that would encourage
  adoption of rainwater harvesting or groundwater recharge practices
